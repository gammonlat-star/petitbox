Our products

petitbox sells limited-run apparel bearing original triangle interpretations of public-domain masterworks, a limited-edition stainless steel sculpture, and personalized commissions built from a photograph you provide. Each piece is made to order — nothing is held in ready-made inventory.

Pricing and payment

Prices are shown in US dollars throughout the site. At checkout, the total is converted to Peruvian soles at the exchange rate in effect at that time, and charged in soles through Mercado Pago, our payment processor. petitbox does not receive or store your card details.

Order confirmation and production

Once your payment is confirmed, your piece enters production — nothing ships from stock. Your certificate of authenticity is generated and emailed to you separately, roughly two weeks after your purchase. We'll reach out by email if anything about your order needs confirming before it goes into production.
